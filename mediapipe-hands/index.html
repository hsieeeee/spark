So what is MediaPipe?

MediaPipe is an open‑source framework from Google for running ready‑made ML "solutions" (hands, faces, body pose, objects…) in real time on phones, laptops and in the browser. The Hand Landmarker used here is a small pipeline of two neural networks. The steps light up as they run on your video:

01

Camera frame

Each video frame from your webcam is handed to MediaPipe, around 30 times a second.

02

Palm detector

A first network scans the whole image and draws a box around any palm. Palms are easier to find than whole hands, because they're rigid and square‑ish.

03

21 landmarks

A second network looks only inside that box and predicts 21 knuckle and fingertip points in 3D (x, y and depth), plus whether it's a left or right hand.

04

Our geometry

MediaPipe stops there. This page's own code measures the points: which fingers are straight, where they point, which way the palm faces, and whether it's moving.

05

Gesture!

Simple rules turn those measurements into an emoji. Plain if/else, no extra AI.
